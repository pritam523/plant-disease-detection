# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is an academic study shared for educational and research use. We
expect all participants to be respectful, accurate, and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Treat contributors and users with courtesy.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions focused on research clarity, correctness, and usability.
- Respect differing levels of experience with Python, web apps, and botany.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information or secrets.
- Deliberate misrepresentation of results or project provenance.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
project-related communication.

## Reporting

Please report unacceptable behavior privately through GitHub's security
advisory form for this repository. Reports will be handled confidentially and
may result in a warning, temporary block, or permanent ban from participation.
